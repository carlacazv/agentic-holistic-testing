## Why

Scripted plans cannot cover every emergent product risk. A session-based exploratory capability must make investigation, coverage, evidence confidence, and defect reproduction durable without turning observations into unverified claims.

## What Changes

- Add independently callable `holistic-qa:explore` instructions.
- Require a charter, timeboxed notes, heuristic selection, coverage notes, evidence index, and reproducible local defect folders.
- Enforce browser/permission/environment gates and local-only defect drafting.
- Add deterministic session validation, rendering, fixtures, and Codex packaging.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `explore-skill`: Evidence-backed session-based exploratory testing and local defect reproduction.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

Adds session contracts, renderer, schema, skill, tests, artifacts, and documentation, using the existing browser and safety foundation.
